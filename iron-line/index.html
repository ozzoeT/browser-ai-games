How to play

1. DeployPick a unit at the bottom (or keys 1–8), then click or drag inside your blue zone. Choose a brush to place a line or a block at once. RemoveRight-click a unit, or use the Erase tool. 2. FightPress Start battle (B). Your troops march and engage on their own. CameraRight-drag to rotate, middle-drag or WASD to move, wheel to zoom, Q/E to turn. On touch: drag to rotate, pinch to zoom. FollowDuring the battle, click a soldier to follow them. Space pauses, and the speed buttons give you slow motion. Take controlPress F while following one of your soldiers: WASD to move, mouse to aim, click to strike or shoot, Esc to leave. TipsSpearmen stop knight charges. Shields stop arrows from the front. Archers and catapults need a wall of men in front of them. Knights hit hardest at full gallop.

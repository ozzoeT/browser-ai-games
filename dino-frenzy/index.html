How to play

Player 1

WSChange lane
DJump (hold = higher)
ADuck (in the air: dive down)
ETurbo
QUse item

In 1-player mode the arrow keys also work, Space to jump and Shift for turbo.

Player 2

IK / ↑↓Change lane
L / →Jump
J / ←Duck
O / EnterTurbo
U / Right ShiftUse item

🎮 Gamepad: d-pad/stick = lanes, A jump, B duck, X/RT turbo, Y/LB item. Esc or P = pause.

Obstacles & tricks

  • Jump logs, boulders, mud and lava. Clear them by a whisker for a PERFECT! and extra turbo.
  • Duck under vines and icicles.
  • Ramps: while airborne press Jump to pull a trick. Land mid-spin and you'll crash!
  • 🥚 Golden eggs fill your turbo, ❓ mystery eggs give an item: Shield, Roar, Super Turbo, Resin.
  • The more obstacles you clear in a row, the faster your FLOW makes you.
  • Jump on a rival's head to stun them!
  • Press Jump exactly on GO! for a perfect start.

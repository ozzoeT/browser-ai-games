How to play

MoveWASD walk · Shift run · mouse to look. Click the game to grab the mouse, Esc to pause. FightLeft click strike or stab. With a bow, hold to draw and release to shoot. ShieldHold Right click (or Space) to raise your shield. It blocks blows and arrows from the front, but every block costs stamina. When stamina runs out your guard breaks! ShoveE or F bash with your shield (or kick): pushes enemies back and drains their stamina. Orders1 Shield wall (your men lock shields beside you) · 2 Follow me · 3 Charge · 4 Hold this spot. War cryQ your squad shouts: stamina back and more courage. Shield wallSoldiers with raised shields standing side by side block much better. Spears stab from the second rank. Hits from the side or back always land! MoraleSquads that lose too many men run away. Break the enemy, keep your line. Fallen?If you fall, you take the place of another soldier of your squad. CampWin battles to earn silver. Spend it on armour, weapons, shields, drill and more men.
